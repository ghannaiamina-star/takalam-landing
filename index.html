HONEST ANSWERS

Questions you
probably have.

Will I actually reach B2 in 18 months?

If you do the work, yes. Students who complete the program as designed — all cycle assessments, weekly self-study, attendance — reach confirmed B2. Our guarantee commits us to that. Students who skip the self-study reach B1. Students who skip everything stay where they are.

Why groups of six specifically?

Because that's the largest group size where communicative teaching actually functions. Research on intensive language programs shows that at 6-8 learners, individual speaking time reaches 20-30 minutes per session. At 15-20, you listen to others speak while you stay silent. That's not language learning. That's an audience.

What if I miss a session?

Life happens. Your teacher shares session notes with anyone who misses. You rejoin the next session. Session audio is available for review.

Do I need to be in Tangier?

No. Takalam is 100% online. We have students across Morocco, from Nador to Laâyoune.

What if my level is below A2?

We'll tell you at the placement interview and recommend Takalam Start when it launches, or a bridging resource in the meantime. We don't accept students into Foundation who aren't ready. It protects you and the group.

What about IELTS or TOEFL?

Foundation prepares you for Cambridge B2 First as the natural endpoint. If you need IELTS or TOEFL specifically, on a deadline, Takalam Private is built for that. We'll assess which one fits you at the interview.

What if I lose motivation halfway through?

The cycle assessments every 8 weeks are designed specifically to prevent this. Students who see their own progress don't quit. Most Takalam learners report that by month 4, they can feel the change in their speaking, and by month 8, other people notice it.

Can I switch between Takalam programs later?

Yes. Students in Foundation sometimes need a short Private sprint for a job interview or specific deadline, then return to Foundation. Foundation graduates commonly move into Advanced. The programs are designed to connect.
