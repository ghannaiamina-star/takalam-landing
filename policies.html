Terms & Policies

Last updated: 7 September 2026.

Payments

Payment is made directly by bank transfer or PayPal before sessions begin. Upload your payment screenshot during registration -- we verify it by hand and confirm your spot by WhatsApp or email, usually within 24 to 48 hours.

Cancellation & refunds

You can cancel at any time, in two clicks. If you've attended two sessions or fewer, you get a full refund within 14 days. Further along than that, message us -- we'll work out a fair solution rather than apply a rigid rule.

Rescheduling

Private: reschedule free of charge with at least 24 hours' notice. Speak: sessions follow the group's fixed schedule and aren't made up individually, which is exactly why the price is low.

Session packs

Packs of 3, 5, or 10 Private sessions don't expire on a fixed date. If your schedule changes, message us and we'll work out a pace that fits.

How sessions work

Private is one to one, 50 minutes, and you book your own slot. Speak is small groups aiming for 10 learners, 90 minutes, twice a week, in breakout pairs and trios so everyone actually speaks. Both run over Zoom or Google Meet -- the link is sent before each session. Be ready a few minutes early; camera on is welcome but not required.

Conduct & recording

Treat every session with respect. Sessions are never recorded by either side without both people's consent. Repeated no-shows or disruptive behavior may affect future bookings.

Communication

WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach us, and we aim to reply within 24 hours on business days. Email works too for anything formal.

Questions about any of this? Message us on WhatsApp or email Takalamenglishcenter@gmail.com.
