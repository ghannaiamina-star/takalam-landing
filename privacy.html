Privacy & data

Last updated: 2026.

This page is a working placeholder covering the minimum required before the free speaking test records anyone's voice. It has not had a legal review. Replace this notice once it has.

What we record

When you take the free English level test, we record your spoken answers, transcribe them automatically, and generate a written assessment of your English level from that transcript and recording.

Why

To assess your spoken English and recommend the right course. Nothing else.

How long we keep it

Recordings and transcripts are kept for 12 months from the date of the test, then deleted automatically. If you are under 18, this is reduced to 6 months, and a parent or guardian must consent before the test is taken.

Who sees it

Your test results and recordings are visible only to Takalam's teacher, for the purpose of your assessment and coaching. They are never sold, shared with advertisers, or used in marketing without your separate, explicit consent.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data or ask us to delete it at any time by messaging Takalam on WhatsApp or emailing Takalamenglishcenter@gmail.com. A fully self-serve download/delete option in your account is planned but not live yet.

Payments

Payments are processed by our merchant of record, who collects and remits VAT and issues your invoice. We do not store your card details.
